Marry Me Chicken
3-4 chicken breasts
2 tbsp olive oil 
2 tbsp unsalted butter (save 1 tbsp for the end)
1/2 yellow onion diced 
5 garlic cloves 
2 tbsp tomato paste
1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes 
1/2 cup dry white wine
1 cup chicken stock
1/3 cup heavy cream 
1 tsp dried oregano
1/2 tsp dried chili flakes 
12-14 basil leaves
2 tsp Dijon mustard 
3/4 cup parmesan cheese 
Squeeze of lemon
Salt and pepper to taste
